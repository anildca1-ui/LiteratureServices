**MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on [DATE] between:

1. **[YOUR LEGAL NAME]**, sole proprietor, trading as CN Document Desk,
   residing at [ADDRESS], Hyderabad, Telangana ("**First Party**"); and
2. **[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]**, a [company/LLP/firm] with its registered office
   at [ADDRESS] ("**Second Party**").

**1. Purpose.** The parties wish to explore and conduct engagements in which
the Second Party discloses technical subject matter for document
procurement, standards retrieval and supplementary search services, and each
party may disclose Confidential Information to the other.

**2. Confidential Information** means all non-public information disclosed
by either party, including search briefs, technical concepts, claim text,
matter context, pricing, methods and deliverables, whether or not marked
confidential. It excludes information that is public through no fault of the
recipient, already lawfully known, independently developed, or lawfully
received from a third party.

**3. Obligations.** Each party shall (a) use Confidential Information only
for the Purpose; (b) not disclose it to any third party except as permitted
in clause 4; (c) protect it with at least reasonable care.

**4. Permitted disclosure.** The First Party may disclose **abstracted
technical concepts only** — never the Second Party's identity, matter name
or commercial context — to sub-processors (native-language reviewer,
document suppliers) and to AI processing tools, in accordance with the First
Party's Data Handling Statement, which forms part of this Agreement.

**5. Compelled disclosure.** Disclosure required by law is permitted with
prompt notice to the disclosing party where lawful.

**6. Return / destruction.** On written request, each party shall delete or
return the other's Confidential Information, save one archival copy where
required for legal or professional-record purposes.

**7. Term.** Obligations run for [3] years from each disclosure.

**8. No licence, no warranty of results.** Nothing grants IP rights.
Search deliverables are supplementary and non-exhaustive per their scope
statements.

**9. Governing law and jurisdiction.** Laws of India; courts at Hyderabad,
Telangana.
