# Outreach base template (for D5)

The `<my_template>` block the D5 prompt asks for. Under 150 words when
personalised; subject under 8 words. The ask is vendor-list registration,
not a sale.

---

**Subject:** Chinese document sourcing — vendor registration

Dear [CONTACT NAME],

[PERSONALISED OPENING LINE — one specific, real observation about their
practice that explains why Chinese-language coverage matters to them.]

I run a Chinese technical-document procurement and verification desk in
Hyderabad. For firms like yours I do three things your team can't easily do
from India: retrieve journal articles and patents from CNKI/Wanfang through
legitimate channels, supply Chinese industry standards including superseded
versions with a verified authenticity chain, and run native-keyword utility
model sweeps that English/MT searching misses.

I'm not asking for a project — I'd like to be registered on your vendor
list. To make that decision easy, I'll do your first job at cost, as an
audition, under mutual NDA with a written data-handling statement.

Would a 15-minute call next week suit?

Regards,
[NAME]
[PHONE] · Hyderabad

---

**Do not send without:** verified contact name and role, a real personalised
opening line, and your approved rate card ready for the reply.
