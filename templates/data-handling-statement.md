# Data Handling Statement — CN Document Desk

> DRAFT — review, adjust to match your actual practice, then it ships with
> every NDA. Never promise a control you don't actually operate.

**Who we are.** CN Document Desk is a sole-proprietor Chinese
technical-document procurement and verification service based in Hyderabad,
India. Because we are a small operation, our confidentiality model is built
on **minimising what leaves the desk**, not on enterprise infrastructure —
and we put that model in writing so you can audit it.

## 1. The abstraction rule (core control)

Your brief is never processed, transmitted or discussed in identifiable
form. Before any part of it reaches an AI tool, a reviewer, or a supplier,
we strip: your company name, matter or case name, litigation posture,
opposing parties, and any commercial context. What remains is the technical
concept only.

## 2. AI tools

- Abstracted technical concepts may be processed with US-hosted AI tools
  (Anthropic Claude) under our paid account.
- **No client-derived text is ever pasted into Chinese-hosted AI tools**
  (DeepSeek web, Qwen, or similar). Those tools are used only for generic
  terminology generation unconnected to any brief.

## 3. Human sub-processors

- Our part-time native-language reviewer receives only: an abstracted
  keyword list and shortlisted **published** documents. Never your brief,
  never your identity.
- Document suppliers (databases, libraries, procurement contacts) receive
  only bibliographic identifiers of published documents — never why we want
  them or for whom.

## 4. Storage and retention

- Working files are kept on an encrypted local disk; client deliverables and
  briefs are not uploaded to shared cloud folders.
- On completion, briefs are deleted on request; deliverables and our search
  logs are retained [12] months for professional-record purposes, then
  deleted.

## 5. Provenance

Every document we deliver states where and how it was obtained. We do not
use circumvention tools or grey-market channels; if a document cannot be
obtained legitimately, we decline the job and say so.

## 6. Breach notification

If we become aware that your information was accessed or disclosed contrary
to this statement, we will notify you within [48 hours] with what we know.
