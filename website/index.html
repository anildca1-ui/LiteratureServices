A procurement and verification desk — not a translation service. We obtain Chinese journal articles, patents and standards through legitimate channels that Indian buyers cannot easily access, and we verify provenance, dates and legal status against the primary source before anything reaches you.

Services

Retrieval

Document retrieval

Journal articles from CNKI (China Academic Journals) and Wanfang, Chinese patent full texts, and CNIPA register extracts — each delivered with a provenance note stating exactly where and how it was obtained, and bibliographic details checked against the source.

Standards

Standards desk

Chinese industry standards — YY (medical), HG (chemical), NY (agriculture), QC (automotive) and others — including superseded and historical versions, with an authenticity chain: source, issuing body, and status on the official Chinese standards platform at the date of delivery.

Landscape

Standards landscape

Which Chinese standards apply to your product: mandatory GB, recommended GB/T and industry series, with mandatory-vs-voluntary flags, interaction with NMPA or other approval pathways where relevant, and a prioritised acquisition list. Every standard number verified before it reaches you.

Search

Native-keyword patent sweep

A supplement to your own English-language search: Chinese utility models and journal literature searched with native technical vocabulary — including the workshop terms machine translation misses — validated by a native-language reviewer, with publication dates verified against the CNIPA register.

How we work

  • Legitimate supply only. No circumvention of access restrictions, no grey channels. Provenance is the product.
  • Verified, or flagged. Every date, legal status and bibliographic field is checked against the primary source. What we cannot verify, we mark as unverified — we do not smooth it over.
  • Your brief stays yours. Client identity and matter context are stripped before any processing; reviewers and suppliers only ever see abstracted technical concepts and published documents. Mutual NDA and a written data-handling statement come first, unprompted.
  • Restrained by design. Deliverables carry a scope statement listing the databases searched, date ranges and classification codes used.
