**CN Document Desk** · Hyderabad · Chinese technical-document procurement & verification

## T1 — Document Retrieval

Single-document procurement from Chinese sources: CNKI / Wanfang journal
articles, Chinese patent full texts, CNIPA register extracts.

- **Price:** Rs 2,500 – 5,000 per document (band depends on source and length)
- **Turnaround:** 48–72 hours
- **Payment:** 100% advance, all clients, no exceptions
- **Includes:** the document as issued by the source; bibliographic
  verification (title, authors/applicant, journal/publication number, dates
  checked against the primary source); a provenance note stating exactly
  where and how the document was obtained
- **Excludes:** theses, conference proceedings, statistical yearbooks and
  census data (restricted for overseas access since April 2023). Where a
  lawful route exists these are quoted separately as library document
  delivery via NSTL / National Library of China; otherwise declined.

## T2 — Standards Desk *(lead product)*

Chinese national and industry standards with verified status.

- **Scope:** industry series (YY medical, HG chemical, NY agriculture,
  QC automotive, and others), **superseded and historical versions**, and
  current GB/GB/T where needed
- **Price:** Rs 10,000 – 25,000 per engagement
- **Turnaround:** 3–7 working days (superseded/historical may take longer —
  confirmed at quote)
- **Includes:** the standard document; authenticity chain (source, issuing
  body, status on the official Chinese standards platform at date of
  delivery); supersession history where relevant
- **Note:** current GB/T English downloads are commodity items available
  elsewhere; this desk's value is industry series, historical versions and
  verified status — that is what the price covers.

## T2+ — Standards Landscape *(highest-value item)*

Which Chinese standards apply to your product, with status flags.

- **Price:** Rs 30,000 – 50,000 per product/landscape
- **Turnaround:** 7–10 working days
- **Includes:** applicable standard families (GB mandatory / GB/T recommended /
  industry series) with reasoning; mandatory-vs-voluntary flags; interaction
  with NMPA or other regulatory approval where relevant; every standard
  number verified on the official Chinese standards platform before delivery;
  a prioritised acquisition list

## T3 — CN Recall Sweep *(sold by demo, never by description)*

Native-Chinese-keyword utility model + journal sweep, positioned as a
**supplement** to the client's own English/MT search — the value is the
recall delta and register-verified dates.

- **Price:** Rs 15,000 – 18,000 per sweep
- **Turnaround:** 5–7 working days
- **Includes:** native keyword set (AI-generated, native-reviewer validated);
  CNIPA classification-code driven search; triage of all hits; deep read of
  top candidates; native-reviewer final read of the top 5–8 documents;
  publication dates verified against the CNIPA register; method note showing
  what native-language searching surfaced that English/MT would likely miss
- **Positioning:** supplementary search. See scope statement below.

## Scope statement (appears on every deliverable)

> Searches were conducted on the databases, date ranges and classification
> codes stated in the deliverable. This is a supplementary search — not a
> guarantee of exhaustiveness.

## Confidentiality

Mutual NDA offered proactively before any brief is shared. Written
data-handling statement available on request. Client briefs are abstracted
before any AI-assisted processing; the full brief is never sent to the
native reviewer or any mainland contact.
